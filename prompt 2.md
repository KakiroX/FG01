## H.3 Figures

- Vector format (SVG or PDF), not PNG screenshots of matplotlib defaults — journals reject or downsample raster figures, and you'll want to relabel axes for the final layout anyway.

- One figure = one finding. Don't let the implementer hand back 13 dashboard-style multi-panel figures (one per SIM); ask for figures built around the RQ-indexed document instead (e.g., "viability boundary," "cost vs. competitors," "P_hit vs. range" — each a candidate Fig. 1,2,3...).
